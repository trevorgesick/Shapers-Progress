NEW 2026-07-19 — this was a REAL AIM BUG, reported from play as "he aims at the top of the capsule but it fires way off", with close range worse than long. FirePoint was at local (0, 0.5, 1.2): 1.2 m in front of the body and 0.825 m below the camera pivot, on a yaw-only root (so it never pitched — aim up and the muzzle stayed put). The aim math was never wrong — AimDir is (crosshairPoint − muzzle).normalized, which correctly converges on the crosshair — but the muzzle and camera were in such different places that the shot LEFT along a line wildly divergent from the one the crosshair draws. With _cameraOffset (0.85, −0.075, −1.36) and pivot _headHeight 1.4, camera−muzzle was (0.85, 0.825, −2.56), giving a launch error of atan(1.184 / (R − 1.2)): 56° at 2 m, 33° at 3 m, 23° at 4 m, 14° at 6 m, 9.9° at 8 m — the 1/(R−1.2) curve the testers described as "a triangle where further shots are more accurate". Also the sole cause of the "fireball looks like it's curving" report: tracking a close target, the 1.2 m lever arm swings the launch angle far faster than the crosshair moves.
